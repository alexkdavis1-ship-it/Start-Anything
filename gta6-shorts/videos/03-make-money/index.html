YOU WILL BE BROKE
You are going
to be broke
in GTA 6.
Reviewers all
say the same
thing.
Money is hard
to come by.
So here is
where the cash
actually is.
One.
Gas station
stick-ups.
Small money,
low effort.
Two.
Gang hideouts.
The game calls
them illegal
operations.
Clear one out
and you leave
with cash,
drugs and
high-value
items.
Three.
Don't sit on
the drugs.
Sell them to
a fence.
Four.
Jewelry stores.
Scout first.
Cut the alarm
before you smash
a display,
and the cops
never get
called.
Five.
Erase the
security tapes,
and the police
don't get your
description.
The big bank
heists unlock
later.
Until then,
hideouts are
your paycheck.
