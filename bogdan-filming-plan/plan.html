How this
works

  1. Film the list. Each video has its own page. The first line is written word for word. Everything after it is bullet points. Talk through them like you would on a call.
  2. Upload everything raw. Every take, mistakes included. Don't trim, caption, or filter anything.
  3. We edit and send it back. You approve within 24 hours, then it goes live.

Four rules on set

  • Say the video number first. "Video 9, take 2." Then a 1-second pause, then the hook.
  • Hook three times. Three different deliveries of the first line, then go straight into the rest.
  • Start talking the moment you hit record. Pauses and stumbles get cut, so keep going.
  • Under 60 seconds per video. If you go long, we cut it down. Don't restart.
  • Film the sessions in order. Session 1 is Black Friday content, so it can't wait.

Last page

Never on camera

  • Client names. Say the niche: "a pet brand," "a supplement brand."
  • Prices, retainers, or plan tiers.
  • Anything that says "send fewer emails."
  • Numbers that didn't happen, or anything that sounds like a guarantee.
  • Unblurred Klaviyo accounts. Film them anyway, we'll blur.
  • "27 flows" and "35 flows" in the same sentence.
  • Unsubscribe numbers as a win, or any partner payouts.
  • "500+ audits". Say "hundreds of audits."
