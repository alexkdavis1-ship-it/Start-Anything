How you say it
changes it

Same sentence. Stress a different word and it means something else.

Say it likeThey hear

I didn't hit his wife.Flat denial.
I didn't hit his wife.I did something else to her.

I didn't hit his wife.I hit someone else of his.

Pause before the big part

"There's something (pause) I really have to tell you."
Don't speed-run it.

Under 3 seconds

Say your hook out loud with a timer. Over 3 seconds? Cut words.

Talk the instant you hit record

TikTok starts recording a beat late. If you wait, then talk, the video opens on dead air, and dead air gets scrolled.

Format · Skits

Two kinds
of skits

1 · POV skit

usually no talking
"POV: your manager is watching"
Think of a frustration everyone's had. Act it out and exaggerate it until it's funny. No crew needed, but it takes creativity. Stuck? Ask ChatGPT for relatable situations.
Honest take: not the best fit for most business accounts.

2 · Two-character skit

talking · you play both
One character is the official (cop, gate agent, clerk). The other is a regular person who knows the rules. The useful info comes out inside the conversation, never said to the camera.
GATE AGENT
"Your flight's cancelled. Best I can do is tomorrow."
YOU
"If I don't take the rebooking, I'm owed a full cash refund, right? Not a voucher."
GATE AGENT
"...Let me process that."
Works for: tickets, citations, landlords, the DMV, contracts.

Formats · Follower magnets

Algo hacks

Short, repeatable, and they convert followers. Bad, Better, Best was the go-to.

Bad, Better, Best

Look up. Point left: "Bad." Middle: "Better." Right: "Best." Three times. Then to camera: "Follow for more."

"Follow these people"

"If you suck at reading..." point up "...follow these people." Repeat with money, AI, listening.

Hire or Fire

Someone off camera with a mic reads a work situation. You answer: "Hire." or "Fire."

Myth or Truth

Someone off camera reads a statement. You answer: "Myth." or "Truth."

Format · Needs a 2nd person

Street
interview

One person holds the mic, one person films. Street, gym, anywhere people are. Takes the most courage. Here's the exact script so it doesn't feel weird.

  1. Walk up: "Hey, question for you. Would you be opposed to being in a TikTok? We do ___."
  2. If yes: "Cool. Pretend you don't know me."
  3. Walk away.
  4. Camera person hits record.
  5. Walk back up and start the video like it's the first time.
Your first question is your hook. Make it something people have an opinion on. Same 3-second rule.
